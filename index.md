Hey there, I am Nicolas! I am a PhD student in the [Department of Theoretical Physics](https://www.unige.ch/sciences/physique/theorique/en) at the [University of Geneva](https://www.unige.ch/en/), working in the group of Prof. [Julian Sonner](https://fiteoweb.unige.ch/~sonner/).<br>

My research lies at the intersection of **quantum** and **statistical mechanics**, with a particular focus on **condensed matter** and **many-body physics**. I am primarily interested in the **nonequilibrium dynamics** of many-body systems, **operator dynamics**, and **quantum chaos** and **integrability**. I also explore **classical chaos** and **nonlinear dynamics**, which emerge as the **classical limit** of the quantum systems I study.

Previously, I received my bachelor's and master's degrees in physics from the [Université libre de Bruxelles](https://www.ulb.be/en), where I spent my final year working under the supervision of Prof. [Pierre Gaspard](https://gaspard.pierre.web.ulb.be/).

Besides conducting research, I am also involved in teaching. Since the beginning of my PhD, I have been a teaching assistant for <i>Group theory for physics students</i> [Spring 2023], <i>Mécanique classique pour mathématicien·nes</i> (Classical mechanics for mathematicians) [Fall 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026], and <i>Méthodes numériques pour physiciens I</i> (Numerical methods for physicists I) [Spring 2026].
